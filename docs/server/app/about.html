The idea

Different skills, one shared point of view.

Arden & Shear began with one chair and a simple idea: good barbering and good color belong together. The shop is bright, a little noisy at the right moments, and built around conversation.

We like clear plans, honest recommendations, and results that make sense for real life—not just the mirror on the way out. Whether it's a regular tidy-up or a change you've been thinking about for months, we make room to get it right.
